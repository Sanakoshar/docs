The Course Management section is where you organise everything your students need for a subject — from lecture notes to assignment deadlines.

## Viewing Your Assigned Courses

After logging in, go to **My Courses** to see all subjects assigned to you for the current semester. Each course card shows student count, current week, and pending actions.

## Uploading Course Materials

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Course Workspace">
    Click on the course name to open the course workspace.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Navigate to Upload">
    Go to **Materials → Upload**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the File Type">
    Select the file type — **Lecture Notes**, **Assignment**, **Reference**, or **Video Link**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter File Details">
    Enter the title, week number, and an optional description.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload the File">
    Click **Upload**. Students can access the file immediately from their portal.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Posting Announcements

Go to the course workspace → **Announcements → New Announcement**. Enter your message and click **Post**. Students receive a portal notification and email.

## Creating Assignments

Under **Assignments → New Assignment**, set the title, description, submission deadline, and maximum marks. Students submit directly through the portal.

## Reviewing Submissions

Go to **Assignments → Submissions** to view, download, and grade student submissions. Use the inline grade entry to award marks.

<Tip>
  Keep course materials organised by week number to help students follow the syllabus progression.
</Tip>
