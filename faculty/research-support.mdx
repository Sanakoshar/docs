NIMS University actively supports faculty research through seed funding, laboratory access, journal subscriptions, and assistance with external grant applications. Whether you are launching a new project or preparing a publication, dedicated resources and staff are available to help you at every stage.

## Research Funding

NIMS offers an internal seed grant of up to **₹2,00,000 per project** to help you initiate new research. Submit your application through **Faculty Portal → Research → Internal Grant Application**. The Research Committee reviews all applications on a quarterly basis, so plan your submission ahead of the review cycle.

## External Grants

The Research Office actively assists faculty with proposals for major funding bodies, including **DST, DBT, ICSSR, and UGC**. From identifying suitable schemes to co-authoring the application, the office provides end-to-end guidance. Contact [research@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:research@nimsuniversity.org) to get started or to discuss an ongoing application.

## Laboratory Booking

Shared research labs can be booked through **Faculty Portal → Research → Lab Booking**. When creating a booking, select the lab, date, preferred time slot, and purpose of use. All bookings must be made **at least 48 hours in advance** to allow adequate preparation time.

## Journal Access

NIMS subscribes to a wide range of academic databases so you can access the literature you need without additional cost:

| Publisher / Platform | Access Method |
|---|---|
| Springer | Library → E-Resources |
| Elsevier | Library → E-Resources |
| IEEE Xplore | Library → E-Resources |
| JSTOR | Library → E-Resources |

Access these resources on campus or remotely via VPN at [library.nimsuniversity.org](https://library.nimsuniversity.org). If you encounter access issues, contact [library@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:library@nimsuniversity.org).

## Publication Support

The Research Office provides several services to help you prepare and publish your work:

- **Plagiarism check (Turnitin)** for manuscripts — request by emailing [research@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:research@nimsuniversity.org).
- **Article Processing Charge (APC) reimbursement** for articles published in UGC-CARE listed journals, up to **₹15,000 per article**.
- **Statistical analysis support** from the Data Science department for quantitative research.

<Tip>
  Register your ORCID iD at [orcid.org](https://orcid.org) and link it to your faculty profile. A verified ORCID iD improves your research visibility and is increasingly required by funding agencies and journals.
</Tip>
