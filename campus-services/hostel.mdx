NIMS University provides on-campus hostel accommodation for students who need a safe, comfortable place to live during their studies. The campus has separate hostel blocks for male and female students, each managed by resident wardens.

## Room Types

Choose from three room types when applying. All fees include electricity and water; mess charges are billed separately.

| Type | Occupancy | Monthly Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Standard Room | 3 sharing | ₹4,000 |
| Semi-Private Room | 2 sharing | ₹5,500 |
| Private Room | Single | ₹8,000 |

## Applying for Hostel Accommodation

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to the Hostel section">
    Log in to the portal and navigate to **Campus Services → Hostel**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your room preference">
    Choose your preferred room type and hostel block. Blocks are assigned separately for male and female students.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your application">
    Submit your application **before the semester start date** to be considered for allotment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay and confirm your room">
    Pay the hostel fee once your room is allotted. Your allotment letter is available under **Hostel → My Room**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Hostel seats are limited. Early applications are given priority.
</Note>

## Mess Facilities

Breakfast, lunch, and dinner are served daily in the central mess. The monthly mess charge is **₹2,800**, billed separately from your room fee. To plan your meals, view the weekly menu under **Hostel → Mess Menu**.

## Hostel Rules

To ensure a safe and disciplined environment for all residents, the following rules apply:

- **Visitor hours** — Visitors are not permitted inside the hostel after **9:00 PM**.
- **Biometric attendance** — Biometric check-in and check-out at the hostel gate is mandatory for all residents.

Violations of hostel rules may result in disciplinary action as per the university's code of conduct.

## Maintenance Requests

If you experience an issue in your room or common area — such as broken furniture or an electrical fault — report it under **Hostel → Raise Maintenance Request**. The maintenance team targets resolution within **48 hours** of a request being submitted.
