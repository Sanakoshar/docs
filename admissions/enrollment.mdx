---
title: "Finalise Your Enrollment at NIMS University After Admission"
sidebarTitle: "Enrollment"
description: "Finalise your NIMS University enrollment after receiving your offer letter — confirm details, pay fees, and activate your student account."
---

Once you receive your admission offer, you must complete enrollment within the deadline specified in the offer letter to secure your seat. Follow the steps below to finalise your enrollment and get ready for the academic year.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Accept the Offer">
    Log in to the portal, go to **Admissions → My Application**, and click **Accept Offer**. This confirms your intent to join the programme and initiates the enrollment process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Enrollment Fee">
    Navigate to **Fees → Pay Now** and pay the semester fee in full. Save your payment receipt — you will need it during document verification.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Physical Documents">
    Bring your original documents and attested copies to the Admissions Office during the scheduled document verification session. Refer to the [Requirements](/admissions/requirements) page for the full list of documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Activate Student Account">
    After your documents are verified, your student account will be activated. You will receive your university email address and student ID number, which you will use throughout your programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attend Orientation">
    Check the portal for your orientation schedule under **Academics → Events**. Orientation introduces you to campus resources, faculty, and your academic calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Failing to complete enrollment by the deadline specified in your offer letter forfeits your seat. You will need to re-apply for a future intake, and your application fee will not be refunded.
</Warning>

<Note>
  Students admitted through management quota should contact the Admissions Office directly at admissions@nimsuniversity.org for specific enrollment procedures and timelines.
</Note>
