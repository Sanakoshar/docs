NIMS University offers a wide range of academic programmes across multiple disciplines, designed to equip students with both theoretical knowledge and practical skills. Whether you are beginning your higher education journey or advancing your career, you will find a programme tailored to your goals.

## Undergraduate Programmes

- **B.Tech** (7 specialisations)
- **B.Sc**
- **B.Com**
- **B.A**
- **BBA**
- **BCA**
- **B.Pharm**

## Postgraduate Programmes

- **M.Tech**
- **MBA**
- **M.Sc**
- **M.Com**
- **M.A**
- **MCA**
- **M.Pharm**

## Diploma & Certificate Programmes

- **Diploma in Engineering** (multiple trades)
- **PG Diploma in Management**
- **Certificate Courses in IT and Languages**

## Doctoral Programmes

NIMS University offers a **Ph.D.** programme in select departments, giving you the opportunity to pursue original research under expert faculty guidance. Applications are accepted **twice yearly** — refer to the admissions portal for current intake cycles and eligibility criteria.
