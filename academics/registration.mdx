Course registration opens before each semester and lets you select the subjects you will study. You must register within the window to be included in the timetable and examination schedule. Missing the deadline can delay your academic progress, so make sure you complete registration on time.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Log In to the Portal">
    Sign in at [nimsuniversity.org](https://nimsuniversity.org) with your student credentials.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Course Registration">
    Navigate to **Academics → Course Registration**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Your Subjects">
    Choose subjects from the list of available electives and compulsory papers for your programme and semester.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check Prerequisites">
    The system flags any subject requiring a prerequisite you haven't completed. Resolve these before confirming.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Registration">
    Review your selection and click **Confirm Registration**. You will receive a confirmation email once your registration is successfully submitted.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Compulsory subjects are pre-filled. You only need to choose electives.
</Note>

<Warning>
  The registration window typically closes **7 days before the semester starts**. Late registration attracts a **₹500 fine** and requires department approval.
</Warning>

## Changing Your Subjects

You can modify your elective choices during the **add/drop period** (first 7 days of the semester). To make changes, go to **Academics → Course Registration → Modify**, update your subject selection, and confirm your revised choices. Changes are not permitted after the add/drop period closes.
