NIMS University's support team is here to help with any question — whether it's a technical issue with the portal, an academic query, or a financial concern. Use the channels below to get in touch or resolve your issue as quickly as possible.

## Raise a Support Ticket

For non-urgent issues or queries that require documentation, raising a ticket is the most effective way to get help.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket form">
    Log in to the portal and navigate to **Help → Raise a Ticket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select a category">
    Choose the category that best describes your issue: **Academic, Financial, Technical, Hostel, Library,** or **Other**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe your issue">
    Write a clear description of your issue and attach any relevant screenshots or supporting documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and note your ticket number">
    Submit the ticket. You will receive a **ticket number** by email for your reference.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track your ticket">
    Monitor the status of your ticket at any time under **Help → My Tickets**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Frequently Asked Questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I reset my portal password?">
    Go to [nimsuniversity.org/login](https://nimsuniversity.org/login) and click **'Forgot Password'**. Enter your registered email address and follow the reset link sent to your inbox.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="My hall ticket is not generating — what should I do?">
    Ensure all outstanding fees are cleared under **Fees → Pay Now**. If your fees are cleared and the issue persists, raise a **Technical** support ticket so the team can investigate.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="How do I update my contact details?">
    Go to **My Profile → Edit Profile**. Update your mobile number or address and click **Save**. Email address changes require additional verification before they take effect.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Who do I contact for placement-related queries?">
    Email [placements@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:placements@nimsuniversity.org) or visit the **Training & Placement Cell** located in the Academic Block.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
