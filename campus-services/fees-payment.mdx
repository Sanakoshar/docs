All fee payments at NIMS University are processed securely through the student portal. You can pay tuition, hostel charges, examination fees, and other dues online at any time — no need to visit any office or counter.

<Warning>
  Unpaid fees block access to hall tickets, transcripts, and course registration. Clear all dues before semester deadlines to avoid disruption to your studies.
</Warning>

## Paying Your Fees

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the payment screen">
    Log in to the portal and go to **Fees → Pay Now**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review your dues">
    Your current outstanding dues are listed by category: **Tuition, Hostel, Examination,** and **Miscellaneous**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select fees to pay">
    Select the fees you want to pay and click **'Proceed to Payment'**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose a payment method">
    Select your preferred payment method: **Net Banking, UPI,** or **Debit/Credit Card**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete and confirm">
    Complete the payment. A receipt is generated instantly and emailed to your registered address.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Payment Deadlines

Keep track of the following deadlines to avoid late payment penalties:

- **Tuition fees** — Due by the end of the **first month** of each semester.
- **Hostel charges** — Due monthly by the **10th of each month**.
- **Late payment penalty** — A **2% monthly penalty** is applied to any outstanding amount past the due date.

## Downloading Receipts

You can access a full record of your payments at any time. Go to **Fees → Payment History** to view and download receipts for all transactions. Keep these receipts for your records and for any future reimbursement or scholarship claims.

## Fee Refund Policy

Overpayments are refunded within **15 working days** of a verified request. Refunds for programme withdrawal follow the university's stated refund schedule, available at [nimsuniversity.org/refund-policy](https://nimsuniversity.org/refund-policy).

<Note>
  NIMS University does not accept cash payments for tuition or hostel fees. All payments must be made through the online portal.
</Note>
