The NIMS University student portal is your central hub for every academic and administrative task throughout your time at the university. From registering for courses and paying fees to borrowing library resources and raising support tickets, everything you need is organised into clearly labelled sections so you can find it quickly and get things done without visiting multiple offices.

## Dashboard

When you log in, the **Dashboard** is the first thing you see. It gives you an at-a-glance summary of upcoming deadlines, unread notifications, and quick links to the services you use most often. Keep an eye on the announcements panel here — important university updates and deadline reminders are posted directly to your dashboard.

## My Profile

The **My Profile** section holds all of your personal information, including your contact details, programme enrolment, and uploaded documents. You can update your address and phone number here, and submit any required documents such as mark sheets or ID proofs directly through the Documents tab.

## Academics

The **Academics** section is where you manage everything related to your studies. Use it to register for courses each semester, view your class timetable, check your exam schedule, and access your results and grade reports once they are published.

## Fees

The **Fees** section gives you a complete picture of your financial account with the university. You can view your current dues, make payments online, download receipts, and check the status of any scholarship or fee waiver that has been applied to your account.

## Library

The **Library** section connects you to the university's digital catalogue and e-resources. Browse and search for books and journals, access e-library subscriptions, and review your borrowing history — all without needing to visit the physical library counter.

## Hostel

If you live on campus, the **Hostel** section lets you view your room allocation, check the weekly mess menu, and submit maintenance requests for any issues in your room or common areas. Responses to maintenance requests are tracked here so you can follow up if needed.

## Support

The **Support** section is your go-to place whenever you need help. You can raise a support ticket for academic or administrative queries, use the live chat feature to speak with a support agent, or browse the FAQs for quick answers to common questions.

<Tip>
  Use the **search icon** in the top navigation bar to quickly jump to any section of the portal. Just type the name of the service or feature you're looking for and select it from the results — no need to navigate through menus.
</Tip>
