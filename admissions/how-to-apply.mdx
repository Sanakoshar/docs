---

NIMS University accepts applications online year-round for undergraduate, postgraduate, and diploma programmes. The process is straightforward — you can complete your application from anywhere, at any time, using the student portal.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose Your Programme">
    Visit [nimsuniversity.org/programmes](https://nimsuniversity.org/programmes) and select the course you want to apply for. Review the programme details, duration, and eligibility criteria before proceeding.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for an Account">
    Create a portal account (see Account Setup) or log in if you already have one. Your account is your central hub for managing your application and tracking its status.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill the Application Form">
    Go to **Admissions → Apply Now**. Complete all sections of the form: personal details, academic history, and programme choice. Ensure all information matches your official documents.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload Supporting Documents">
    Attach scanned copies of all required documents. See the [Requirements](/admissions/requirements) page for the full list of documents you need to provide.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Application Fee">
    Pay the non-refundable application fee of **₹500** online via net banking, UPI, or card. Your application will not be submitted until payment is confirmed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit and Track">
    Click **Submit**. Note your Application ID — use it to track your status at any time under **Admissions → Track Application**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  You will receive an email confirmation within 24 hours of submitting your application. If you do not receive it, check your spam folder or contact the Admissions Office.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Apply at least 30 days before the programme start date to avoid missing the intake. Early applications also give you more time to arrange funding and accommodation.
</Tip>
