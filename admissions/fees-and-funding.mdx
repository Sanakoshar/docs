NIMS University offers transparent fee structures and several funding options to make quality education accessible. Whether you are applying for a scholarship, an education loan, or a government scheme, you can find all the information you need here.

## Programme Fees

The table below outlines the annual tuition fees and other charges for each programme category.

| Programme | Annual Tuition Fee | Other Charges |
|---|---|---|
| B.Sc / B.Com / B.A | ₹45,000 | ₹8,000 |
| B.Tech / B.E | ₹95,000 | ₹12,000 |
| MBA / M.Tech | ₹1,10,000 | ₹15,000 |
| Diploma Programmes | ₹25,000 | ₹5,000 |

<Note>
  Fees are subject to change each academic year. Always confirm the current fee schedule on the portal under **Fees → Fee Structure** before making any payment.
</Note>

## Scholarships

NIMS University provides several scholarship schemes to support deserving students:

- **Merit Scholarship:** Awarded to students who scored 85% or above in their qualifying examination. This scholarship covers up to 50% of your annual tuition fee.
- **Need-Based Scholarship:** If you require financial assistance, apply under **Admissions → Scholarship Application** and submit your income proof along with the application.
- **Government Schemes:** NIMS University is registered on central and state government scholarship portals, including the National Scholarship Portal (NSP) and various state-specific portals. You can apply through these portals using your admission details.

## Education Loans

If you need to finance your education through a loan, NIMS University can help connect you with the right lender:

- NIMS has tie-ups with several nationalised banks offering education loans at competitive interest rates.
- Visit the **Finance Office** on campus or contact [finance@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:finance@nimsuniversity.org) to request a referral letter for your chosen bank.

<Tip>
  Apply for scholarships early — the Merit Scholarship deadline is typically **30 days after the admission offer**. Missing this window means waiting until the next academic year.
</Tip>
