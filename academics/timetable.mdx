Your personalised timetable is generated automatically once you complete course registration. It shows your classes, lab sessions, and tutorial timings for the week, giving you a clear view of your schedule before the semester begins.

## Viewing Your Timetable

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Timetable Page">
    Log in and navigate to **Academics → Timetable**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Browse Your Weekly Schedule">
    Your weekly schedule is displayed by day and time slot, covering all registered subjects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View Class Details">
    Click on any class to see the **room number**, **faculty name**, and **subject code** for that session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Downloading Your Timetable

Click the **Download PDF** button in the top-right corner of the timetable page to save a printable copy. You can share this file or print it for quick offline reference throughout the semester.

## Timetable Changes

Faculty substitutions and room changes are posted under **Academics → Notices**. You will also receive an **SMS** and a **portal notification** whenever a change affects one of your registered classes, so you are never caught off guard.

<Tip>
  Add the timetable to your phone's calendar by clicking **Add to Calendar** — this downloads an `.ics` file compatible with Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, and Outlook.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Timetables are finalised **one week before each semester**. Check back if you see **'Timetable Not Yet Published'** — it means finalisation is still in progress.
</Note>
