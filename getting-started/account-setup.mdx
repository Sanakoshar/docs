---
title: "Create and Activate Your NIMS University Portal Account"
sidebarTitle: "Account Setup"
description: "Create your NIMS University portal account step by step — from registration to verifying your email and logging in for the first time."
---

All NIMS University services — from course registration and fee payments to exam results and library access — are accessed through the student portal. Setting up your account is straightforward and takes only a few minutes, so you can get started with everything the university has to offer right away.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Visit the Portal">
    Open your browser and go to [nimsuniversity.org](https://www.nimsuniversity.org). On the homepage, click the **Register** button to begin creating your account.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Your Details">
    Fill in the registration form with your full name, date of birth, email address, and mobile number. Make sure the email address you provide is active, as it will be used for all official university communications.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify Your Email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from NIMS University. Open the email and click the confirmation link to verify your address. If you don't see it within a few minutes, check your spam or junk folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a Password">
    Choose a strong password for your account. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include at least one uppercase letter and one number. Avoid using easily guessable information such as your name or date of birth.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Log In">
    Return to [nimsuniversity.org](https://www.nimsuniversity.org) and click **Sign In**. Enter your registered email address and the password you just created to access your student portal for the first time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you are a new admission student, use the **Application ID** found in your offer letter as your initial login credential instead of a password. You will be prompted to set a permanent password on your first login.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Bookmark the portal URL in your browser so you can access it quickly during busy registration periods without having to search for it each time.
</Tip>
