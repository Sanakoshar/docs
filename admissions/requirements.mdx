Each programme at NIMS University has specific academic eligibility criteria. Check your eligibility carefully before beginning your application to ensure you meet the minimum requirements for your chosen programme.

## Undergraduate Programmes

To be eligible for an undergraduate programme at NIMS University, you must have:

- Passed 10+2 (or equivalent) from a recognised board with a minimum **50% aggregate**.
- Met any specific subject requirements for your chosen programme (e.g., Science stream for Engineering). Check the individual programme page for details.

## Postgraduate Programmes

To be eligible for a postgraduate programme at NIMS University, you must have:

- A Bachelor's degree from a recognised university with a minimum **50% aggregate**.
- An undergraduate background in a relevant discipline for specialised programmes (e.g., an engineering degree for M.Tech).

## Diploma Programmes

To be eligible for a diploma programme at NIMS University, you must have:

- Passed 10th standard (SSC or equivalent) for most diploma courses.
- Note that some diploma courses accept candidates who have completed 10+2 or hold a graduate degree — refer to the specific programme page for details.

## Required Documents

Prepare and upload the following documents when you submit your application:

- 10th mark sheet and certificate
- 10+2 mark sheet and certificate (for UG/PG applicants)
- Degree certificate and transcripts (for PG applicants)
- Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar, Passport, or Voter ID)
- Passport-size photographs (2 copies)
- Category certificate (SC/ST/OBC) if applicable
- Migration certificate (if transferring from another board or university)

<Note>
  All documents must be uploaded as clear scanned PDFs not exceeding **2 MB each**. Blurry or incomplete scans may delay the processing of your application.
</Note>
