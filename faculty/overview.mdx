The Faculty Portal is your centralised workspace for all teaching, assessment, and administrative tasks at NIMS University. Everything from uploading course materials to submitting grade sheets is handled here.

## Getting Access

Your portal credentials are provided by the HR Department on your first day. Use your staff email to log in at [nimsuniversity.org/faculty](https://nimsuniversity.org/faculty). Contact IT Support if you face login issues.
