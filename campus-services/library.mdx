The NIMS University library offers both a physical collection and a digital resource hub, giving you access to textbooks, journals, research papers, and multimedia content. Whether you're on campus or studying remotely, the library equips you with the resources you need to succeed.

## Physical Library Hours

The physical library is open during the following hours:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday – Saturday | 8:00 AM – 8:00 PM |
| Sunday | 10:00 AM – 4:00 PM |

## Borrowing Books

Use your student ID card to borrow up to **4 books** at a time. Each loan period lasts **14 days** and can be renewed once through the portal. To renew, log in and navigate to **Library → My Borrowings → Renew**.

## Digital Resources

Log in to the portal and go to **Library → E-Resources** to access the full suite of digital materials. Your student account gives you access to:

- **JSTOR, Springer, and Elsevier** journal archives
- **NIMS institutional repository** — theses and dissertations
- **NPTEL and Coursera** course materials
- **DELNET consortium** digital library

## Renewing and Returning Books

To renew a book online, go to **Library → My Borrowings → Renew** before your due date. Return physical books to the library desk during opening hours. Late returns incur a fine of **₹5 per day per book**, so make sure to return or renew on time.

## Requesting New Titles

If a book you need isn't in the collection, you can suggest it. Submit your request under **Library → Suggest a Book**. The library team reviews all suggestions monthly and adds titles based on academic relevance and demand.

<Tip>
  Download the NIMS Library mobile app — available on **iOS and Android** — to renew books and access e-resources from anywhere, without needing to log in through a browser.
</Tip>
