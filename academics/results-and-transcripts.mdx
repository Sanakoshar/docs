Your examination results are published on the portal within **30 days** of the last exam. You can view subject-wise marks, your **SGPA** (Semester Grade Point Average), and your cumulative **CGPA** from the Results section — all in one place.

## Viewing Your Results

Navigate to **Academics → Results**. Select the **academic year** and **semester** to see your marks and grades for every registered subject. You can also download a copy of your grade sheet as a PDF for personal records.

## Grade Point System

NIMS University uses a 10-point grading scale:

| Grade | Marks Range | Grade Points |
|---|---|---|
| O | 90–100 | 10 |
| A+ | 80–89 | 9 |
| A | 70–79 | 8 |
| B+ | 60–69 | 7 |
| B | 50–59 | 6 |
| C | 40–49 | 5 |
| F | Below 40 | 0 |

## Requesting Transcripts

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Transcripts & Certificates">
    Navigate to **Academics → Transcripts & Certificates**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select the Document Type">
    Choose from:
    - **Unofficial Transcript (PDF)** — for personal or informal use
    - **Official Transcript (sealed)** — for university applications or employers
    - **Degree Certificate** — issued after completing all programme requirements
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Copies and Delivery">
    Select the number of copies and your preferred delivery method: **download** (for PDFs) or **post** (for sealed/physical copies).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay the Applicable Fee">
    Official copies cost **₹200 per copy**. Unofficial PDF transcripts are **free of charge**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Await Dispatch">
    Official transcripts are dispatched within **7 working days** of your request being confirmed.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Revaluation

If you wish to contest a result, apply for **revaluation** within **15 days** of result publication under **Academics → Results → Apply for Revaluation**. The fee is **₹500 per subject**. Your answer sheet will be re-evaluated by a different examiner, and the updated result (if any) will be reflected on the portal.

<Note>
  **Degree certificates** are issued at the annual convocation ceremony. If you need proof of completion sooner, a **provisional certificate** is available on the portal immediately after you pass all programme requirements.
</Note>
