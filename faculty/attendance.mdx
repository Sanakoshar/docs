NIMS University requires students to maintain at least 75% attendance in each subject to be eligible for end-semester examinations. As faculty, you are responsible for marking attendance after every class.

## Marking Attendance

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Mark Attendance">
    Log in and go to **Attendance → Mark Attendance**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select Course and Session">
    Select the course, date, and session (**Theory** / **Lab** / **Tutorial**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Student Status">
    The student roster is displayed. Mark each student as **Present (P)**, **Absent (A)**, or **On Duty (OD)**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save Attendance">
    Click **Save**. Attendance is recorded in the system immediately.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Editing Attendance

You can edit attendance for the current week until the end of Saturday. Go to **Attendance → Edit Attendance**, select the date and session, and make corrections.

## Viewing Attendance Reports

Go to **Attendance → Reports** and select a course to see cumulative attendance percentages for all enrolled students.

## Shortage Alerts

Students who drop below 75% receive an automatic alert. You can view the list of at-risk students under **Attendance → Shortage Alerts** and issue a notice.

<Note>
  On Duty (OD) attendance is granted for university-approved events. Students must produce an OD letter signed by the HoD.
</Note>

<Tip>
  Mark attendance within 30 minutes of the class ending to ensure accurate records.
</Tip>
