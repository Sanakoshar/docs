Whether you are a new applicant, a current student, or a faculty member, you can manage every step of your university journey right here. From applying for admission to accessing campus services, the portal puts everything you need at your fingertips.

## Getting Started in Four Steps

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create Your Account">
    Register on the NIMS University portal with your personal details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Apply or Enroll">
    Submit an admission application or complete your semester enrollment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for Courses">
    Browse the timetable and select your subjects for the term.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Access Campus Services">
    Pay fees, book hostel accommodation, and use the digital library.
  </Step>
</Steps>

  Need help? Contact our support team at support@nimsuniversity.org or visit the Help & Support page.
