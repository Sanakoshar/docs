The NIMS University IT department provides all teaching and research technology services. Your single set of credentials gives you access to email, the Learning Management System, licensed software, and campus networks — keeping your digital workspace unified and straightforward to manage.

## Your University Email

Your staff email address follows the format **firstname.lastname@nimsuniversity.org**. Access your inbox through Outlook Web App at [mail.nimsuniversity.org](https://mail.nimsuniversity.org), or configure it on any desktop or mobile email client using **Microsoft 365** settings. Your IT credentials are the same as your faculty portal login.

## Learning Management System (LMS)

NIMS uses **Moodle** as its LMS. Log in at [lms.nimsuniversity.org](https://lms.nimsuniversity.org) using the same username and password as the faculty portal. Course shells are created automatically at the start of each semester, so your enrolled students will have access from day one.

## Licensed Software

The following tools are available at **no cost** to all faculty members:

| Software | Purpose |
|---|---|
| Microsoft 365 (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Teams) | Productivity and collaboration |
| MATLAB and Simulink | Engineering and scientific computing |
| SPSS and SAS | Statistical analysis |
| Turnitin | Plagiarism detection |
| Adobe Acrobat Pro | PDF editing and management |

To request installation on a university device, go to **IT → Software Request** in the Faculty Portal.

## Campus Wi-Fi

Connect to the **'NIMS-Faculty'** network using your faculty portal credentials. This network provides full access to on-campus resources and the internet. Do not share your Wi-Fi credentials with students; a separate student network is available for their use.

## IT Support

If you experience a technical issue, raise a support ticket through **Faculty Portal → Help → Raise a Ticket** and select **Technical** as the category. You can also email [it@nimsuniversity.org](mailto:it@nimsuniversity.org) directly. Classroom technology issues receive priority response to minimise disruption to teaching.

<Note>
  Password resets can be done self-service through **Faculty Portal → My Account → Reset Password**. If you are locked out and cannot access the portal, call the IT Helpdesk at **extension 1234** for immediate assistance.
</Note>
