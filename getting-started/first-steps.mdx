---

Once your admission is confirmed, there are a few essential tasks you need to complete before the semester begins. Working through these steps early ensures your enrolment is fully processed, your records are up to date, and you are ready to attend classes from day one.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Upload Documents">
    Log in to your student portal and navigate to **My Profile → Documents**. Upload clear, legible copies of your 10th and 12th mark sheets, a valid government-issued photo ID, and a recent passport-size photograph. All files must be in PDF or JPEG format and under 2 MB each.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay Admission Fee">
    Navigate to **Fees → Pay Now** in the portal. Select your programme from the list, review the fee breakdown, and complete your admission fee payment before the deadline displayed on screen. You will receive a payment confirmation email and a downloadable receipt immediately after the transaction.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Collect Your ID Card">
    Visit the **Registrar's Office** on campus with a printed or digital copy of your fee payment receipt. Your student ID card will be issued on the spot and serves as your official university identification for exams, library access, and campus facilities.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register for Courses">
    Go to **Academics → Course Registration** in your portal. Browse the available subjects for your programme and semester, select the courses you wish to enrol in, and confirm your registration. Check with your academic advisor if you are unsure which subjects are compulsory for your programme.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Join Your Classes">
    Once your course registration is complete, head to **Academics → Timetable** to view your personalised class schedule. Note your classroom locations, lecture timings, and any lab or tutorial sessions so you are prepared from the very first day of term.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Course registration closes **7 days before the semester starts**. Missing this window may delay your enrolment by a full semester, so make sure you register well before the deadline.
</Warning>
