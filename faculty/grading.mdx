The Grading section of the Faculty Portal is where you enter, review, and submit marks for all assessments. Marks are visible to students after the department releases the results.

## Grade Entry Workflow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select Course and Assessment Type">
    Go to **Grading → Mark Entry** and select the course and assessment type (**IA1**, **IA2**, or **End-Semester**).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Student Marks">
    The student roster for the course is pre-loaded. Enter marks in the score column for each student.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save a Draft">
    You can save a draft at any time before the submission deadline.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit Marks">
    Once all marks are entered, click **Submit Marks**. A confirmation dialog asks you to verify before final submission.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Forwarded to Examination Cell">
    Submitted marks are forwarded to the Examination Cell for processing.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Marks cannot be edited after the submission deadline without a formal correction request to the Examination Cell.
</Warning>

## Grading Scale

| Grade | Range   | Points |
|-------|---------|--------|
| O     | 90–100  | 10     |
| A+    | 80–89   | 9      |
| A     | 70–79   | 8      |
| B+    | 60–69   | 7      |
| B     | 50–59   | 6      |
| C     | 40–49   | 5      |
| F     | \<40    | 0      |

## Revaluation Requests

If a student applies for revaluation, you will receive a notification under **Grading → Revaluation Requests**. Review the paper and enter a revised mark if warranted.

<Note>
  Internal assessment marks are out of 30 per assessment for most programmes. End-semester marks are out of 70.
</Note>
