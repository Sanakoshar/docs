Understanding NIMS University's core policies helps you work effectively and ensures a consistent, fair experience for all students. This page summarises the key policies you need to know, covering academic integrity, attendance, examinations, leave entitlements, and professional conduct.

## Academic Integrity Policy

NIMS University enforces a **strict zero-tolerance policy** on plagiarism for both students and faculty. All submitted work — including assignments, research papers, and project reports — must be original and properly attributed. You are required to run manuscripts through **Turnitin** before submission and to use it when setting take-home examinations for students. Breaches of this policy are subject to disciplinary action under the Employee Handbook.

## Attendance Policy

To ensure adequate instructional coverage, you must:

- Mark student attendance **within 30 minutes** of each class session.
- Conduct **at least 90%** of your scheduled classes per semester.
- Substitute or reschedule any missed classes with **prior departmental approval**.

Persistent non-compliance may be noted in your annual performance review.

## Examination Conduct Policy

When assigned as an invigilator, you must:

- Report to the examination hall **20 minutes before** the scheduled start time.
- Switch off your mobile phone for the duration of invigilation.
- **Immediately report** any malpractice incidents to the **Chief Superintendent** using the prescribed reporting form.

Timely reporting of malpractice is essential to maintain examination integrity and protect the interests of all students.

## Leave Policy

Faculty are entitled to the following categories of leave each year:

| Leave Type | Entitlement | Conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Casual Leave | 12 days | Maximum 3 consecutive days per instance |
| Medical Leave | 15 days | Medical certificate required |
| On-Duty Leave | As applicable | For conferences, FDPs, and sponsored research |

Submit all leave applications through **Faculty Portal → Leave → Apply for Leave**. Your Head of Department (HoD) is the approving authority. Ensure you apply in advance wherever possible to allow cover arrangements to be made.

## Code of Conduct

You are expected to maintain **professional behaviour** in all interactions with students, colleagues, and external stakeholders — whether in person, in writing, or online. For detailed guidelines on expected conduct, grievance procedures, and disciplinary processes, refer to the full **Employee Handbook** available at [nimsuniversity.org/policies](https://nimsuniversity.org/policies).

<Note>
  Policy documents are reviewed **annually**. The Faculty Affairs Office sends email notifications whenever a policy is updated. Ensure your university email is active and monitored so you do not miss important changes.
</Note>
