NIMS University conducts internal assessments throughout the semester and end-semester examinations at the close of each term. You must download your hall ticket to appear in any examination — entry to the exam hall is not permitted without it.

## Examination Calendar

NIMS University follows a structured assessment schedule each semester:

| Assessment | Timing |
|---|---|
| Internal Assessment 1 | Week 5–6 |
| Internal Assessment 2 | Week 10–11 |
| End-Semester Examination | After Week 16 |

Exact dates are published on the portal under **Academics → Exam Schedule** at the start of each semester.

## Downloading Your Hall Ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Hall Ticket">
    Go to **Academics → Examinations → Hall Ticket**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Clear Outstanding Dues">
    Ensure all dues are cleared — unpaid fees will block your access to the hall ticket.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Generate and Download">
    Click **Generate Hall Ticket** and download the PDF to your device.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Bring It to Every Exam">
    Print your hall ticket and bring it to every examination along with your student ID card.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Examination Rules

Follow these rules to avoid disqualification or disciplinary action:

- Arrive at least **15 minutes** before the exam start time.
- **Electronic devices** (phones, smartwatches) are not permitted in the exam hall.
- Bring your **hall ticket** and a **valid photo ID** to every exam.
- Answer sheets are evaluated internally; results are published on the portal within 30 days.

## Missed Examination

If you miss an exam, apply for a **make-up exam** within **3 days** of the missed date under **Academics → Examinations → Make-Up Request**. Medical emergencies require a doctor's certificate to be submitted along with your request.

<Warning>
  Students without a hall ticket will **not** be permitted to sit the examination under any circumstances. No exceptions are made at the venue.
</Warning>
